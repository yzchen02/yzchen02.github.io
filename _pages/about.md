I am a Master's student at [Tianjin University](https://www.tju.edu.cn/), advised by Prof. [Jiale Cao](https://jialecao001.github.io/), and a research intern at **Kling AI**, Kuaishou. I received my B.S. from [Central South University](https://www.csu.edu.cn/) in 2024. I am fortunate to work closely with **Muxi Diao** and [**Xuyang Liu**](https://xuyang-liu16.github.io/).

My current research focuses on **visual coding agents**: enabling multimodal models to turn visual intent into executable programs, editable scenes, and videos. I am particularly interested in how agents understand visual and spatial requirements, use code to build and control a scene, and improve their results through execution, world-state checks, and visual feedback. My work spans agent evaluation, training environments, and post-training with SFT and agentic reinforcement learning.

Recently, I have been working on **[VibePrevisBench](https://vibeprevisbench.github.io/)** for agentic 4D scene generation in Blender and **[VibeVideoBench](https://vibevideobench.github.io/)** for code-driven video generation. Previously, I worked on efficient diffusion language model decoding ([STDec](https://yzchen02.github.io/STDec/)), personalized image generation (InDP), and multimodal foundation models (Keye-VL-2.0).

I am always happy to discuss visual coding agents, multimodal reasoning, and programmatic visual creation. Feel free to reach out at [yzchen@tju.edu.cn](mailto:yzchen@tju.edu.cn).

I build **visual coding agents** at **Kling AI, Kuaishou**. I am a master's student at [Tianjin University](https://www.tju.edu.cn/), advised by Prof. [Jiale Cao](https://jialecao001.github.io/), and work closely with [Xuyang Liu](https://xuyang-liu16.github.io/).

My research asks how an agent can turn a visual brief into code, render a scene, and spot what it got wrong. I build training environments and benchmarks for agents that make videos and direct animated scenes in Blender. Earlier, I worked on multimodal post-training at Kuaishou and handwriting generation at iFLYTEK.
